Gapminder 2018: income and life expectancy by country

Bubble chart of 187 countries in 2018. Horizontal axis: income, GDP per capita in US dollars, on a log scale. Vertical axis: life expectancy in years. Bubble size: population. Colour: world region. The "Life vs. income" view instead colours each country by how many years longer or shorter its people live than its income predicts, using a trend line fitted to all countries. In the Regions view, the legend buttons focus one region and mark its population-weighted average. Type in the search box to highlight matching countries; press Enter to jump to the first match. In the chart, use the arrow keys to move between countries: left and right by income, up and down by life expectancy. Home and End go to the poorest and richest country. Escape clears the selection. The full data is also available in the table below the chart.
